## 🎬 Video Recording Checklist & Preparation

### 1. Browser Setup
* Open **Google Chrome** or **Brave** in Full Screen mode (`F11`).
* Bookmark and open tabs:
  1. Tab 1: `http://localhost:3000/` (Command Dashboard)
  2. Tab 2: `http://localhost:3000/verify` (Outcome Parameter 10.1: Rumor Scanner)
  3. Tab 3: `http://localhost:3000/sentiment` (Outcome Parameter 10.2: Sentiment & Pulse)
  4. Tab 4: `http://localhost:3000/broadcast` (Outcome Parameter 10.3: Multilingual Studio)
  5. Tab 5: `http://localhost:3000/simulation` (Snan Simulator)
* Set zoom to **100%** or **90%** so all cards and radar maps fit cleanly on screen.

### 2. Audio & Microphone Setup
* Use a clear headset or lapel mic to prevent echo.
* Speak in a confident, authoritative tone like a young government technical advisor.
* You can speak either in **fluent English** or a professional **mix of English & Hindi (Hinglish)**. Both options are provided below.

## 💡 Pro Presentation Tips for Hardik:
1. **Pace yourself:** Do not rush. Take a 1-second pause when switching between tabs.
2. **Move the mouse deliberately:** Do not shake or make erratic mouse movements on screen.
3. **Show, then speak:** Click the button first, let the UI react, and then explain what just happened.
4. **End on the Dashboard:** It looks the most visually impressive and leaves a lasting impression on the examiners.
