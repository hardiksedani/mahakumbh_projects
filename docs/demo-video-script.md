# KUMBHRAKSHAK: Video Presentation & Live Demo Script

### Screenplay & Spoken Narration Guide

---

### [00:00 - 00:45] Scene 1: The High-Stakes Context & Problem Statement
- **Visual Cue:** Start on the **Executive Briefing Page** (`/executive`). Show the title banner *"KUMBHRAKSHAK: Decision Intelligence Brief"* and the provenance badge *"DEMO / SIMULATED DATA"*.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Respected HOD Sir, Faculty Evaluators, and Members of the Committee. Welcome to the technical demonstration of **KumbhRakshak**—an AI Safety Intelligence and Decision Support Platform designed for the upcoming **Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2027 in Nashik–Trimbakeshwar**.*
  >
  > *During peak Shahi Snan days, over 10 million pilgrims gather across tight river corridors like Ramkund and Laxman Jhula. In mass gatherings of this scale, crowd disasters are rarely caused by density alone—they are triggered by sudden panic, such as a viral social media rumor claiming a bridge collapse or stampede. Existing police setups treat CCTV monitoring, social media listening, and field dispatch as separate, disconnected silos. KumbhRakshak was built to bridge these silos into a unified, evidence-based AI decision layer."*

### [01:45 - 03:00] Scene 3: Outcome Parameter 10.1 — AI Rumor Scanner & Evidence Matrix
- **Visual Cue:** Navigate to **10.1 Rumor Scanner** (`/verify`). Show the claim: *"STAMPEDE at Gate 7!! Everyone running!!"*. Show the **Evidence Matrix** comparison:
  1. Social Signal: 4 clustered reports.
  2. Ground Camera Evidence: CAM-782 & CAM-RK-01 show normal queue flow (0% counter-flow).
  3. Recycled Media Check: No historical match.
  4. Final Verdict: **CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED**.
  Click the **"Generate Multilingual Counter-Advisory"** button.
- **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"Now let's examine Outcome Parameter 10.1: AI-Powered Misinformation and Rumor Detection.*
  >
  > *Suppose a viral panic post appears on social media claiming a stampede at Gate 7. In traditional operations, verifying this requires phone calls and radios, taking 15 to 30 minutes. In KumbhRakshak, our verification engine automatically queries nearby ground cameras within a 2-kilometer radius using Haversine distance, applies distance-decayed Bayesian evidence fusion, and discovers that adjacent cameras show completely normal, calm queue movement.*
  >
  > *The system classifies the claim as CONTRADICTED with 92% confidence. Instantly, our Multilingual Counter-Messaging engine generates verified official counter-advisories in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and English: 'Official Alert: Normal pilgrim movement at Gate 7. Do not forward unverified rumors.' Stopping panic in under 2 seconds."*

Show the node progression: Report $\rightarrow$ Proximity $\rightarrow$ Bayesian Fusion $\rightarrow$ 10-Factor Risk Score $\rightarrow$ Officer SOP. - **Spoken Narration:**
  > *"A cornerstone of KumbhRakshak is transparency and human governance. We never use an autonomous black-box AI. Look at this visual Evidence Lineage Graph:*
  >
  > *The system tracks the full chain of reasoning: from the initial ingested report, to the spatial proximity query, through Bayesian cross-camera fusion, into our explainable 10-factor causal risk engine.*
  >
  > *The system recommends a Standard Operating Procedure—in this case, dispatching Quick Response Team 4 and triggering an automated Ghat PA announcement chime. However, the action CANNOT execute autonomously. A commanding officer must authenticate with their badge ID. When Officer Shinde clicks 'Authorize', an immutable audit record is logged to our governance database. Full accountability is maintained."*
